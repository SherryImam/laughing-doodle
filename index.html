Sales Lead · Emi & Co (e-commerce)

2022 – 2025

Problem: 90% of signed-up affiliates drove zero sales, with no CRM, onboarding or per-partner tracking.

What I did: led sales across the store; cut the affiliate program by 70% to partners who fit; set acceptance criteria and tiered commission; built promo kits and a CRM with unique partner links.

Result (affiliate rebuild across both brands): +40% affiliate revenue, −25% cost per sale and 4x return on commission in 3 months; the top 20% of partners drove 75% of revenue.
