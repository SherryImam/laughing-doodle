Tiers & guardrails

Bronze

30% recurring commission · partner kit (hooks, video scripts, email swipes) · monthly tips email

Silver · 5+ customers

Everything in Bronze + $100 quarterly bonus · featured in our partner directory

Gold · 15+ customers

Everything in Silver + co-marketing (joint webinar, newsletter swap) · early access to features

Guardrails: applications reviewed against the target audience (freelancers, agencies, accountants) · no paid search on brand terms · partners with no activity in 90 days are moved to inactive and offered a restart call.
