Trial sign-up campaign

Goal: free-trial sign-ups at under $10 each. Three audiences tested against each other, with budget moved to the winners after day 7. This example is on Meta; I run the same test-and-scale method on TikTok Ads, Google Ads and other platforms.

Campaign structure

Campaign: Quillbook · Trial sign-ups (Leads objective)
Ad set 1 · Cold: freelancers & agency owners, 22–45, interests: freelancing, invoicing, Upwork
Ad set 2 · Lookalike: 1% lookalike of paying customers
Ad set 3 · Retargeting: landing page visitors, last 30 days, no sign-up

Tracking: Meta Pixel + Conversions API, UTM tags on every ad, sign-ups synced to the CRM.

Optimisation log

  • Day 3: paused 2 of 6 creatives with CTR under 1%
  • Day 7: moved 25% of cold budget to the lookalike ad set (lower cost per sign-up)
  • Day 12: new retargeting creative using the "get paid in 6 days" review
  • Day 20: tested a "free invoice template" hook against "get paid 2x faster"; the "2x faster" hook won by 31%
